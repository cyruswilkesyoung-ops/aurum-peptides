COA Documentation: A Complete Reference

Research Guides· auraa Laboratory Desk· April 7, 2026· 9 min read

This is the long-form companion to our shorter verification guide: a field-by-field reference for the Certificate of Analysis, plus a method for archiving documentation so any batch remains traceable long after it ships.

Header fields

  • Compound name and any synonyms.
  • Batch / lot number — the anchor for everything else.
  • Manufacture and test dates.
  • Testing laboratory and, ideally, an accreditation reference.

Analytical fields

  • HPLC purity — percentage plus the chromatogram.
  • Mass spectrometry — observed vs theoretical molecular weight.
  • Appearance and physical form (e.g. white lyophilized powder).
  • Water content or residual solvents where relevant.

Archiving for traceability

Store each COA under its batch number, cross-referenced to your receiving date and internal sample IDs. When documentation is organized by batch — as on our Certificates page — a sample used months later can still be tied back to its original identity and purity data.

Good archiving is what makes a COA useful in year two, not just on the day of delivery. Batch is the key; everything else hangs off it.
