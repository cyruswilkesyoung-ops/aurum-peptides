Temperature-Controlled Storage: Best Practices

Useful· Aurum Laboratory Desk· March 18, 2026· 5 min read

Temperature control is the most controllable variable in research-compound handling — and therefore the one most worth systematizing. This is a practical, receiving-to-freezer walkthrough.

At receiving

Inspect packaging on arrival, record the date and condition, and move lyophilized material to its designated storage promptly. Note any transit temperature indicators if present.

Storage tiers

  • Long-term stock — frozen, sealed, dark.
  • Working stock — refrigerated, minimizing door-open time.
  • Reconstituted samples — refrigerated and aliquoted to avoid repeat freeze–thaw.

Documenting the cold chain

A storage log that records temperature tier, batch and dates converts routine handling into traceable data. Pair it with the batch COA — see the Certificates page — for a complete record from synthesis to storage.

The goal is not perfection but documentation: a record that shows, for any sample, how it was held and for how long.
